## What it checks

WCAG 1.2.2 requires that captions are provided for all prerecorded audio content in synchronized media (video with audio), except when the media is a media alternative for text and is clearly labeled as such.

## How it works

The Node engine scans the DOM for `<video>` elements and evaluates the presence of captions:

1.  **Exemption Check**: Muted videos (no audio track) and content identified as "live" (via `data-live`, HLS URLs, or "stream" in the source) are skipped as they fall under other criteria.
2.  **Native Tracks**: Checks for `<track>` elements with `kind="captions"` or `kind="subtitles"` that have both `src` and `srclang` attributes.
3.  **ARIA Alternatives**: Validates if `aria-describedby` points to a text block that serves as a text alternative.
4.  **Nearby Evidence**: Scans the surrounding DOM for `<details>` blocks or links containing "caption" or "transcript" keywords.
5.  **Cross-Origin Detection**: Identifies cross-origin video players (like YouTube or Vimeo iframes).

## Status Semantics

*   **PASS**: A valid `<track>` element or clear text alternative is found.
*   **FAIL**: No captions or alternatives are detected for a non-muted prerecorded video.
*   **INCOMPLETE**: Emitted for cross-origin iframes (YouTube/Vimeo) where the auditor cannot inspect the internal DOM to confirm captioning.

## Common violations

| Violation | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Missing track | A `<video>` element with audio but no `<track kind="captions">`. |
| Cross-origin uncertainty | A YouTube embed that may have captions, but they cannot be verified automatically. |
| No alternative | A promotional video with music and speech but no visible text version. |

## Suggested fix

Add a valid WebVTT caption track to your video elements.

```html
<!-- Correct implementation -->
<video controls>
  <source src="presentation.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  <track 
    kind="captions" 
    src="captions_en.vtt" 
    srclang="en" 
    label="English" 
    default
  >
</video>
```

For third-party embeds, ensure that the player's captioning feature is enabled by default or provide a link to a full transcript nearby.

## Known limitations

*   **Cross-Origin Restriction**: Same-origin policy prevents the auditor from inspecting the DOM inside YouTube or Vimeo iframes.
*   **Dynamic Injection**: Captions injected via JavaScript after the initial page load may be missed by the static scan.
