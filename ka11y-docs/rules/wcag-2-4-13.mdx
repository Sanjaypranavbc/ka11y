## What it checks

WCAG 2.4.13 (Focus Appearance) requires that the keyboard focus indicator has:
1.  **Sufficient Area**: A minimum area that is at least as large as the area of a 2 CSS pixel thick perimeter of the unfocused component.
2.  **Sufficient Contrast**: A contrast ratio of at least 3:1 between the focus indicator and the background.

## How it works (Unified Pipeline)

The Unified Pipeline performs a rigorous geometric and colorimetric analysis of the focus indicator:

1.  **Interaction Simulation**: `InteractionStateRunner` focuses each element and captures the visual delta.
2.  **Area Calculation**: The engine measures the thickness of the focus ring (supporting `outline`, `box-shadow`, and `border` changes) and compares it against the perimeter-based area requirement.
3.  **Contrast Analysis**: The `ContrastEngine` samples the color of the focus indicator and the background color (including resolved backgrounds from upward DOM traversal) to compute the relative luminance.
4.  **Policy Evaluation**: `Policy2413` evaluates both the area and contrast independently.

### False Positive Protection

*   **Inversion Support**: Pure contrast inversion (e.g., swapping background and text colors) is natively supported as a valid focus indicator.
*   **Shadow and Blur Handling**: The engine correctly parses complex `box-shadow` spreads and blurs to determine the "effective" thickness of the indicator.
*   **Resolution of Transparencies**: Correctly handles indicators with partial opacity by blending them with the underlying background color before calculating contrast.

## Node Engine Implementation (`focus-appearance.check.js`)

The Node engine provides a secondary, measurement-focused layer:
1.  **Style Capture**: Measures the `outline-width` and `box-shadow` spread of a focused element.
2.  **Minimums Enforcement**: Flags indicators with a width below 2px or a contrast ratio below 3:1 against the computed background.

## Status Semantics

*   **FAIL**: The indicator is too thin (area requirement) or too faint (contrast requirement).
*   **PASS**: Both area and contrast requirements are satisfied.
*   **NEEDS_REVIEW**: Emitted when the contrast cannot be accurately measured (e.g., against a complex background image).

## Common violations

| Violation | Example |
|-----------|-----------|
| Thin outline | A 1px dotted outline (fails the 2px area equivalent). |
| Faint shadow | A light grey box-shadow on a white background with < 3:1 contrast. |
| Incomplete check | A focus ring appearing over a hero image where the background color is variable. |

## Suggested fix

Use a thick, high-contrast focus indicator.

```css
/* FAILED - Too thin and low contrast */
button:focus {
  outline: 1px solid #ddd;
}

/* PASSED - High visibility */
button:focus {
  outline: 3px solid #000;
  outline-offset: 2px;
}

/* PASSED - Contrast inversion */
a:focus {
  background-color: #000;
  color: #fff;
}
```

## Known limitations

*   **Variable Backgrounds**: Indicators appearing over complex gradients or images may result in `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
*   **3D Transforms**: Indicators that rely purely on 3D transforms or perspective shifts may be missed by the current 2D geometric analyzer.
